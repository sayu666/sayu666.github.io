About

I am studying at XJTLU's Taicang campus. My current interests sit where computer networks meet distributed systems: how a packet actually gets from one machine to another, what guarantees TCP makes that UDP does not, and how work is split across many machines once a dataset stops fitting comfortably on one.

The coursework behind this page covers socket programming, the application and transport layers, the MapReduce model, and machine learning on Spark. Rather than describing those topics in the abstract, this site is the deployed result of one of them.

Spark MLlib

An end-to-end machine-learning workflow on Spark: loading, feature preparation, training, prediction and evaluation.
